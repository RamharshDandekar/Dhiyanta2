RFP-TC-2024-089: Advanced Materials Testing Laboratory - Complete Equipment Package
⏰ URGENT: Proposal Submission Deadline - January 10, 2026, 5:00 PM IST
Client: TechCorp Industries Pvt. Ltd.
Project Code: RFP-TC-2024-089
Industry: Manufacturing & Quality Assurance
Location: Pune, Maharashtra & Bangalore, Karnataka
Budget Range: ₹2.5 - 3.2 Crores (USD 300K - 390K)
Contract Type: Turnkey Project (Supply, Install, Train)

📋 Project Overview

TechCorp Industries, a leading manufacturer of aerospace and automotive components, is seeking qualified vendors to establish state-of-the-art materials testing laboratories at our manufacturing facilities in Pune and Bangalore. This initiative is part of our expansion strategy to meet international quality standards (AS9100D, IATF 16949) and support our growing export business.

The selected vendor will be responsible for end-to-end project execution including equipment procurement, laboratory design, installation, calibration, validation, personnel training, and post-installation support.

🔬 Technical Requirements - Equipment List

Category A: Mechanical Testing Equipment
  • Universal Testing Machine (UTM):
    • Capacity: 100kN (minimum)
    • Testing modes: Tension, compression, flexure, peel, tear
    • Digital control with PC-based data acquisition system
    • Load accuracy: ±0.5% of indicated value
    • Extensometer: Automatic, gauge length 50mm, ±20mm travel
    • Compliance: ISO 6892-1, ASTM E8, ASTM D638
  • Hardness Testing Equipment:
    • Rockwell hardness tester (digital, all scales)
    • Brinell hardness tester (10mm ball, 3000kgf load)
    • Vickers microhardness tester (automated stage, image analysis)
    • Shore durometer (A and D scales for polymers)
    • Calibrated test blocks and indenters included
  • Impact Testing:
    • Charpy impact tester: 450J capacity, digital readout
    • Izod impact tester for plastics
    • Low temperature testing chamber (-70°C to +150°C)
    • Standard specimens and fixtures
  • Fatigue Testing:
    • Servo-hydraulic fatigue testing system
    • Load capacity: ±50kN
    • Frequency: 0.01 to 100 Hz
    • Environmental chamber compatible
Category B: Metallurgical Analysis Equipment
  • Optical Emission Spectrometer (OES):
    • Analysis range: C, Si, Mn, P, S, Cr, Ni, Mo, Cu, Al, V, Ti, Nb, etc.
    • Material coverage: Ferrous and non-ferrous alloys
    • Accuracy: ±0.02% for major elements
    • Sample preparation equipment included
    • Certified reference materials (CRMs) for calibration
  • Metallurgical Microscope:
    • Inverted design with motorized stage
    • Magnification: 50x to 1000x
    • Brightfield and darkfield illumination
    • Digital camera (minimum 5MP) with image analysis software
    • Grain size, inclusion rating, phase analysis capabilities
  • Sample Preparation Equipment:
    • Automated grinding and polishing machine (6 specimens capacity)
    • Precision cutting machine with abrasive wheels
    • Mounting press (hot and cold mounting)
    • Ultrasonic cleaning system
    • Complete consumables kit (abrasives, polishing cloths, etchants)
Category C: Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Equipment
  • Ultrasonic Testing (UT):
    • Digital flaw detector with A-scan, B-scan capabilities
    • Frequency range: 0.5 to 20 MHz
    • Normal and angle beam probes (various angles and frequencies)
    • Calibration blocks (V1, V2, IIW type)
  • Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT):
    • AC/DC magnetization with yoke and prod options
    • UV lamp (365nm, minimum 1000 μW/cm²)
    • Wet and dry magnetic particles (visible and fluorescent)
    • Demagnetization equipment
  • Dye Penetrant Testing (PT):
    • Complete penetrant system (cleaner, penetrant, developer)
    • UV inspection booth with controlled lighting
    • Consumables for 100+ inspections
  • Radiography (Optional - High Value Add-on):
    • X-ray inspection system (if facility permits)
    • 160kV minimum, digital imaging
    • Complete radiation safety infrastructure
Category D: Dimensional Measurement & Quality Control
  • Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM):
    • Measuring volume: 500 x 400 x 400 mm (minimum)
    • Accuracy: (2.5 + L/300) μm
    • Touch-trigger probe with automated tool changer
    • CAD comparison and GD&T analysis software
    • Temperature controlled environment setup
  • Surface Roughness Tester:
    • Parameters: Ra, Rz, Rq, Rt, etc.
    • Stylus type with motorized drive
    • Measuring range: 0.01 to 40 μm Ra
    • Traceable calibration standards
  • Precision Measurement Tools:
    • Digital micrometers (0-25mm, 25-50mm, 50-75mm sets)
    • Digital calipers (0-150mm, 0-300mm)
    • Height gauges (digital, 0-300mm)
    • Gauge blocks (grade 1, 112 piece set)
    • Pin gauges and thread gauges (comprehensive sets)
Category E: Environmental & Special Testing
  • Salt Spray Chamber:
    • Capacity: 1000 liters (minimum)
    • Temperature range: 35°C to 55°C
    • ASTM B117, ISO 9227 compliant
    • Programmable controller with data logging
  • Humidity Chamber:
    • Temperature range: -20°C to +80°C
    • Humidity range: 20% to 98% RH
    • Capacity: 500 liters minimum
    • Programmable cycles and data logging
  • Thermal Shock Chamber:
    • Hot zone: +60°C to +200°C
    • Cold zone: -70°C to +25°C
    • Transfer time: < 10 seconds
    • Basket capacity: 50 liters

⚙️ Additional Requirements

  • Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS):
    • Cloud-based or on-premise LIMS software
    • Integration with all major test equipment
    • Automated report generation and certificate printing
    • Sample tracking, test scheduling, and workflow management
    • Multi-user access with role-based permissions
    • Audit trail and data integrity (21 CFR Part 11 compliant preferred)
  • Laboratory Infrastructure Support:
    • Layout design and equipment placement recommendations
    • Electrical load calculations and panel specifications
    • HVAC requirements for temperature/humidity control
    • Compressed air and gas pipeline specifications
    • Safety equipment (eye wash stations, fire extinguishers, PPE)
  • Calibration & Maintenance:
    • Initial calibration of all equipment (NABL traceable)
    • Calibration certificates valid for 12 months
    • Annual calibration schedule and cost estimate
    • Preventive maintenance plan for first 3 years
    • Spare parts list with pricing

👨‍🏫 Training Requirements

  • Comprehensive training for 15 quality control personnel (per location)
  • Duration: Minimum 5 days hands-on training at each site
  • Topics: Equipment operation, maintenance, calibration, troubleshooting
  • Training materials: SOPs, manuals, quick reference guides
  • Post-training assessment and certification
  • Optional: Advanced training at vendor's facility (2 personnel per location)
