RFP/NHAI/2024/INFRA-001: Automated Highway Testing Equipment Supply & Installation
Issuing Authority: National Highway Authority of India (NHAI)
Project Location: Delhi-Mumbai Expressway (Phase 3)
Tender ID: NHAI-INFRA-2024-001
Published Date: December 1, 2025
Submission Deadline: January 15, 2026
Estimated Project Value: ₹45,00,00,000 (USD 5.5 Million)

Project Scope:

The National Highway Authority of India invites qualified vendors to submit proposals for the supply, installation, and commissioning of comprehensive automated testing equipment for the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway Phase 3 project. This is a critical infrastructure initiative aimed at ensuring world-class highway quality standards.

Technical Requirements:

  • Concrete Testing Equipment:
    • Universal Testing Machine (UTM) - 3000kN capacity, digital control
    • Automatic compression testing machines with data logging
    • Non-destructive testing (NDT) equipment - ultrasonic and rebound hammer
    • Concrete core drilling machines with 150mm dia capacity
  • Asphalt & Bitumen Testing:
    • Automated Marshall Stability testing apparatus
    • Bitumen penetration testing equipment (automatic)
    • Ductility testing machines (digital readout)
    • Flash and fire point testers (Cleveland open cup)
  • Soil Testing Laboratory Equipment:
    • California Bearing Ratio (CBR) testing equipment - automated
    • Proctor compaction testing apparatus (standard and modified)
    • Direct shear testing machines - digital control
    • Triaxial testing equipment with data acquisition
  • Quality Control Systems:
    • Integrated LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System)
    • Real-time data monitoring and cloud backup systems
    • Automated report generation software (BIS compliant)
    • GPS-enabled field testing equipment for on-site quality checks

Mandatory Specifications:

  • All equipment must comply with BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) specifications
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing facility
  • CE marking for electrical equipment
  • Calibration certificates from NABL accredited laboratories
  • Minimum 3 years comprehensive warranty with AMC option
  • Installation at 5 designated highway testing laboratories across 3 states
  • Training for 25 technical personnel (on-site and vendor facility)
  • 24/7 technical support hotline with maximum 48-hour breakdown response time

Deliverables & Timeline:

  • Equipment delivery: Within 90 days of purchase order
  • Installation & commissioning: Within 120 days
  • Personnel training completion: Within 150 days
  • Complete documentation (operation manuals, test certificates): With delivery
  • Spare parts inventory: Minimum 2 years supply at each location

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Minimum 10 years experience in highway infrastructure testing equipment supply
  • Annual turnover: Minimum ₹50 crores in last 3 financial years
  • Prior experience: At least 3 similar projects with government agencies (minimum ₹20 crores each)
  • Valid GST registration and PAN card
  • No blacklisting by any Central/State Government department
  • EMD (Earnest Money Deposit): ₹90,00,000

Evaluation Criteria:

  • Technical Proposal: 70% weightage
    • Equipment specifications compliance: 30%
    • Quality certifications and testing reports: 20%
    • Implementation plan and timeline: 15%
    • Training program quality: 5%
  • Financial Proposal: 30% weightage
    • Competitive pricing
    • Payment terms
    • AMC costs (years 4-10)
